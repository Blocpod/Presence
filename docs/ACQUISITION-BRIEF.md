# PRESENCE — an integration thesis for creator platforms

PRESENCE explores a licensed digital presence that lets creators make approved, personalized interaction available beyond their own working hours. The platform retains its audience, subscriptions and settlement. PRESENCE contributes a runtime connecting authorization, creator policy, isolated fan memory, disclosed interaction and metered usage.

The executive demonstration uses Mira Vale, a fictional adult creator, and fictional adult fans. It is a local sandbox, not an operating creator business. There is no verified real identity, paid customer evidence, live payment settlement or production security certification.

## Why a platform such as OnlyFans should evaluate it

The useful question is whether a creator can provide more valuable interaction while retaining meaningful control over how their identity is used. A governed presence could create additional access inventory, preserve scarce human time for selected conversations and bring continuity to otherwise fragmented interactions. These are hypotheses to test with willing creators and informed fans.

The wedge is narrow: embed a disclosed Presence session beside existing creator messaging and memberships. Accept the platform's signed identity and entitlement claims; return policy decisions and idempotent usage events. Keep existing payments and creator relationships in the platform. No OnlyFans API, partnership or willingness to buy has been established.

## Why now, and what is already available

Fanvue already documents AI messaging and AI/live voice distinctions. Delphi offers personal digital minds. Tavus and HeyGen provide conversational/streaming avatars. These products make a demonstration feasible and also make a generic AI creator chatbot easy to compete with. [Competitive evidence](../RESEARCH.md)

The potential asset is the tested continuity of authorization, creator control, fan-specific memory and commercial permissions across surfaces and providers. That is an engineering and adoption hypothesis, not an established moat. A large platform can reproduce software; our case must be measured integration time, operational reliability and useful outcomes.

## What the prototype can and cannot establish

The local product is designed to let an observer change creator permissions, compare distinct fan relationships, save/delete memory, switch modalities, simulate human takeover, inspect sandbox usage and revoke the presence. Acceptance evidence and actual command/browser results are maintained in [EVALS.md](../EVALS.md) and [BUILD-LOG.md](BUILD-LOG.md). This brief does not substitute planned journeys for executed results.

The deterministic conversation adapter establishes a reproducible governance flow. It does not establish general model quality, photorealistic video, real creator workload savings or provider reliability. Generic browser voice is not a licensed cloned voice. A browser spatial scene is not proof of a shipped AR product.

## Economics to investigate

Use explicit scenario inputs:

`annual gross opportunity = creators × paying fans per creator × adoption × monthly Presence ARPU × 12`

`platform gross share = annual gross opportunity × platform take rate`

`creator gross share = annual gross opportunity − platform gross share`

These are projected gross flows, not profit or valuation. If retention uplift is modeled, show it as a separate sensitivity assumption; do not count the same retained subscriber revenue twice. Pilot contribution margin must deduct provider/media costs, infrastructure, review/moderation, support, refunds and payment costs. Measure displaced existing revenue as well as new revenue. Seeded ledger totals are sandbox activity, not traction.

## Acquire, license or build internally

| Choice | Rational when | Evidence still required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| License | A partner wants a bounded pilot with portable data and replaceable providers. | Integration effort, pricing, responsibilities and termination/export terms. |
| Acquire | The team/runtime reduces time and risk materially, and creator adoption produces an asset the platform values. | Real usage, audited controls, asset ownership, retention and cost evidence. |
| Build | Existing teams can deliver equivalent quality and governance within their own constraints. | Fair comparison against the same feature set and acceptance tests. |

No acquisition price, revenue uplift, proprietary dataset advantage or switching-cost claim is supported yet. The next credible milestone is a consented pilot with a control group, a tested partner sandbox and a measured comparison against an internal baseline.
