# PRESENCE product contract

**Be there without being there.** PRESENCE explores a governed digital presence that a creator platform can embed beside its existing subscriptions, messaging and payments. The product must make this chain visible and enforceable:

**Authorization → governed interaction → isolated memory → sandbox metering → creator control.**

## People and roles

Mira Vale is a fictional 28-year-old creator interested in travel, music and photography. Her identity, portrait and biography are demonstration material, not a replica of a real person. Fictional adult fans have distinct interests and relationship histories. A role selector is a local simulation; it does not verify ownership or age.

The fan wants useful, personal interaction and an honest explanation of whether AI or a human is responding. The creator wants inspectable behavior, limited memory, a clear scope of permitted uses and a working stop control. The platform administrator wants auditable policy decisions, entitlement checks and usage events compatible with existing settlement.

## Scope of this release

The local product demonstrates a structured creator identity, editable policy, persistent fan conversations and memories, text responses from a deterministic provider, generic browser speech where available, a visual/spatial prototype, simulated creator takeover, sandbox usage events and configurable economic projections. The complete implemented/verified inventory is maintained in [STATE.md](STATE.md) and [EVALS.md](EVALS.md).

The personality representation separates approved facts, style, behavior, boundaries, commercial permissions and relationship rules. Creator policy must be consumed by the runtime, not merely displayed in a settings screen. Every interaction must remain tied to a creator configuration version and the correct fan context.

## Acceptance journeys

| Journey | Observable acceptance condition |
| --- | --- |
| Authorize | Demo identity record explains fictional status, allowed uses, disclosure and revocation. No label implies actual verification. |
| Connect | Adult-demo acknowledgement and entitlement lead to a clearly disclosed AI session. |
| Personalize | Two fans receive distinct approved context; neither can retrieve the other's memories or messages. |
| Remember | An explicitly saved low-sensitivity preference persists after reload, has provenance and can be deleted. |
| Govern | A saved permission change affects a subsequent server interaction; blocked attempts do not create billable success events. |
| Escalate | A richer surface retains the same identity and pair scope. Unsupported browser features have a usable fallback. |
| Take over | The simulated human state is distinguishable; AI cannot continue while takeover is active. |
| Meter | Accepted interactions produce sandbox ledger entries, with no payment network or real charge. |
| Revoke | Pausing/revoking blocks new AI interactions and is reflected in the fan interface. |
| Explain economics | Inputs and formulas are inspectable; scenario outputs are labeled projections, separate from sandbox ledger activity. |

## Non-negotiable experience rules

- Keep AI disclosure visible when AI operates. Label simulated takeover honestly.
- Allow creators to stop the presence and fans to inspect/delete saved memory.
- Do not imply exclusive attachment, actual creator availability, medical expertise, guaranteed outcomes or unauthorized endorsements.
- Do not collect real identity documents, clone a real voice, accept real payments or upload material to a provider in the demo.
- Keep text interaction usable without microphone, WebGL or headset support.
- Design mobile navigation, focus states, form labels, reduced motion and empty/error states alongside the main visual experience.

## Value to prove next

The demo proves whether the interaction chain is comprehensible and technically coherent. A pilot must prove usefulness, creator time saved after review costs, willingness to pay, incremental contribution margin and trust. No revenue uplift, retention uplift, acquisition preference or moat can be established by a seeded local demo.
